**Mac beta, community tested.** Draft, not published.

Rough Cut for Mac, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac machines (Apple chip and Intel): each build opened a project, cut it, exported it and the export was compared with the edit before it was attached here.

- `mac-arm64` for Apple chip Macs (M1 and newer), `mac-x64` for Intel Macs.
- Not signed with an Apple developer account: the first launch needs **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway** (see "Opening Rough Cut on a Mac" on the download page).
- Recording needs Screen Recording permission (then reopen the app); microphone and camera are asked for when used.
- Beta limits: main display only, system audio needs a loopback driver (BlackHole), no automatic updates yet, captions transcription runs on the CPU.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS-mac-*.txt`.
