Describe a graphic. Claude draws it over your video.

Type a sentence, and the graphic lands on its own layer over the recording. Move it and resize it like any other clip. Real editor capture, generation wait sped up. Optional: graphics need an eligible Claude subscription.

Every graphic is drawn fresh.

No template library. Each prompt gets its own original design, played live over your recording and kept on its own lane.

One click, a new background.

Colors, gradients and wallpapers sit behind your recording. Pick one and the preview changes on the spot.

One recording, any layout.

Widescreen, vertical story, square or side by side. Screen and camera rearrange themselves; you don't record again.

Let it find the dead air for you.

The AI edit tab scans the recording, lists the silent stretches to cut and suggests where to zoom. You pick what to apply. Real editor capture, analysis wait shortened. Optional: it needs an eligible Claude subscription.

Zoom in on what matters.

Zooms sit on their own lane in the timeline and play back smoothly over the recording.

Cut a stretch. Everything closes the gap.

Remove a range and the screen, camera and sound close up together. Playback runs straight across the cut.

Rough Cut's limits today: Linux x86_64 with X11 (no Wayland), glibc 2.35 or later, and graphics need an eligible Claude subscription. What comes next.

Planned, no dates

  1. Automatic zoom

    Zoom suggestions that follow where the cursor and clicks happen. Today zooms are placed by hand.

  2. Wayland recording

    Record on Wayland desktops. Today Rough Cut needs X11.

  3. More AI models for graphics

    Codex and Antigravity models next to Claude, so graphics do not depend on one subscription.
