Describe a graphic. Claude draws it over your video.

Type a sentence and a graphic lands on its own lane in the timeline, drawn live over the recording. Real editor capture, generation wait not shown. Optional: graphics need an eligible Claude subscription.

Let it find the dead air for you.

The AI edit tab scans the recording, lists the silent stretches to cut and suggests where to zoom. You pick what to apply. Real editor capture, analysis wait shortened. Optional: it needs an eligible Claude subscription.

Zoom in on what matters.

Zooms sit on their own lane in the timeline and play back smoothly over the recording.

Cut a stretch. Everything closes the gap.

Remove a range and the screen, camera and sound close up together. Playback runs straight across the cut.
